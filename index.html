Popular Expressions

Fa waka?

Meaning: “How are you?” One of the most common greetings used daily.

No span

Meaning: “No worries.” A relaxed phrase often used in conversations.

Mi lobi yu

Meaning: “I love you.” Used for family, partners, and close friends.

Yu sabi?

Meaning: “Do you understand?” Frequently used while teaching or explaining.

A no mi

Meaning: “It wasn't me.” A classic Surinamese response used jokingly.

Mi de ete

Meaning: “I'm still here.” Often used casually in conversations.
